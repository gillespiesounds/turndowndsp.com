POSTER BOY

Channel strip

As a producer and session musician, I work with a lot of tracks that these days get grouped and shaped on a bus. But you can’t send bus EQ to a mix engineer when you print multitracks. POSTER BOY lets me EQ from the bus and make everything fit the song as if it were one instrument, knowing that when I print my tracks they’ll have the shape I heard in my session.

Put it on every track in the group and once on the bus, and set the bus one to Master. Use the EQ to shape the bus so it fits the song. Your EQ, AIR and SAT moves are applied to the POSTER BOY on each track.

The compressor, expander, limiter and reverb stay local to each track. Classic tone-shaping tools, so you can mix single tracks the way you would with any other channel strip.

Sometimes, once your tracks are in a song, all they need is a little air or a little density to sit in the mix. AIR pushes the high highs forward as much as you need, without getting harsh or stepping on toes. SAT adds some tape density to fill space in your low mids and give your mix a solid foundation to sit on.

The console lets you change the balance and cohesion of your tracks from one window, so you aren’t opening and closing plugin windows while you’re trying to think about the big picture.

Built with tracking in mind. At default settings it adds 0 ms of latency, so you can record your instrument the way you want to hear it, and it nulls completely against the original track. Your sound to shape, not ours.

Tempo Bridge

Mac app

Your instrument joins the band.

Ableton Link is already great, but it has no hierarchy, which makes it hard to rely on for live performance. Tempo Bridge lets you send and receive the Link information you need without making decisions for you. MIDI tempo is great, but it doesn't give you a grid or a downbeat you can trust. The music is out there. Tempo Bridge brings it to you.
